✦ FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about Wondermayank and this digital universe.

Who is Wondermayank?
Wondermayank is a student developer from India who creates free online tools, exam preparation simulators, movie and anime finders, study blogs and 70+ digital projects hosted at wondermayank.github.io.
What tools does Wondermayank offer?
Wondermayank offers 70+ free tools including a Movie Finder, Anime Finder, Book Finder, Paletteify color palette generator, Text to PDF converter, CBT mock tests for CUET, NEET, JEE, Banking and SSC exams, a Periodic Table explorer, Hindi typing practice, Card Generator and more.
What is the Wondermayank CBT Mock Test?
The Wondermayank CBT Mock Test is a free Computer-Based Test simulator that helps students prepare for CUET, NEET, JEE, Banking (SBI, IBPS) and SSC exams with realistic timed practice sessions. Access it at wondermayank.github.io/cbtmock.
Is wondermayank.github.io free to use?
Yes, all tools and resources on wondermayank.github.io are completely free to use with no account or sign-up required. The entire site is open access and built for students and learners across India.
How can I contact Wondermayank?
You can contact Wondermayank via Telegram at @Mindmaster_robot or join the Telegram community at t.me/wondermayank. You can also follow on YouTube at youtube.com/@wondermayank and Instagram at instagram.com/WONDERMAYANK.
What is Paletteify by Wondermayank?
Paletteify is a free color palette generator tool by Wondermayank available at wondermayank.github.io/paletteify. It helps designers and developers create beautiful, harmonious color palettes instantly — no sign-up needed.
Does Wondermayank have a study blog?
Yes, Wondermayank maintains a study blog at commercesehoga.blogspot.com with structured notes, resources and educational content for commerce students, exam prep aspirants and general knowledge seekers. There is also a ThunderStudy blog at thunderstudyofficial.blogspot.com.
