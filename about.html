Who is Wondermayank?

Wondermayank is a student developer and digital creator from India. The name "Wondermayank" represents a digital universe — a collection of 70+ free tools, study resources, cultural projects and creative experiments, all freely available at wondermayank.github.io.

The mission is simple: make quality digital tools and educational resources freely accessible to every student in India, regardless of their background or resources. Everything on this site is completely free with no sign-up required.

Projects span seven themed categories — Finder (movie, anime, book discovery), Study (exam simulators, notes, current affairs), Tools (PDF converter, color palette generator), Love (card generator), Art, India & Culture, and Entertainment.
