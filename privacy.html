Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 1, 2026 wondermayank.github.io

Overview

Welcome to Wondermayank (wondermayank.github.io). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our website, tools, and services. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our practices.

By using this website, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you disagree with any part, please discontinue use of the site.

Short version: We don't collect personal data, sell your information, or require account creation. This site is free and runs entirely in your browser.

Information We Collect

Wondermayank does not actively collect personally identifiable information (PII). However, the following may be automatically gathered:

  • Browser local storage: Some tools (like CBT exam simulators and ThunderStudy) store your progress data locally in your browser. This data never leaves your device.
  • Usage analytics: We use Google Analytics (GA4) to understand anonymous traffic patterns — pages visited, device type, and geographic region. No personal identity is tracked.
  • Third-party embeds: Embedded content (YouTube videos, Telegram widgets) may collect data per their own privacy policies.

GitHub Pages Hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages (github.io). GitHub may collect server logs including IP addresses, browser type, and referring URLs for security and infrastructure purposes. Please review GitHub's Privacy Statement for details.

Cookies

Wondermayank itself does not set tracking cookies. However, the following may set cookies:

  • Google Analytics: Sets anonymous measurement cookies (_ga, _gid) to track sessions. These do not identify you personally.
  • Google Fonts: May log requests to serve fonts. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Theme preference: We store your dark/light mode choice in your browser's localStorage — not a cookie, and not sent to any server.

Third-Party Services

Our site integrates with these third-party services, each governed by their own policies:

  • Google Analytics 4 — anonymous traffic measurement
  • Google Fonts — web font delivery
  • YouTube — embedded video content
  • Telegram — community links and bot contact
  • TMDB / Jikan APIs — movie and anime data for finder tools
  • Google Sheets (Apps Script) — used in ThunderStudy exam result recording

We do not control these services. Please review their respective privacy policies before use.

Children's Privacy

Wondermayank is designed to be a safe, educational resource. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has submitted personal information through our site, please contact us immediately via Telegram at @Mindmaster_robot.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
