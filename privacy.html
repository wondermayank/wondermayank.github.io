Overview

Welcome to Wondermayank (wondermayank.github.io). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our website, tools, and services. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being fully transparent about our practices.

By using this website, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you disagree with any part, please discontinue use of the site.

✅ Short version: We don't collect personal data, sell your information, or require account creation. This site is free and runs entirely in your browser.

💬

Information We Collect

Wondermayank does not actively collect personally identifiable information (PII). However, the following may be automatically gathered:

  • Browser local storage: Some tools (like CBT exam simulators and ThunderStudy) store your progress data locally in your browser. This data never leaves your device and is inaccessible to us.
  • Usage analytics: We use Google Analytics (GA4) to understand anonymous traffic patterns — pages visited, device type, and geographic region. No personal identity is tracked.
  • Third-party embeds: Embedded content (YouTube videos, Telegram widgets) may collect data per their own privacy policies.

🛡️ Local-only promise: All study progress, scores, and preferences are stored in your browser's localStorage. This data is never transmitted to any server and is invisible to us.

🖥️

GitHub Pages Hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages (github.io). GitHub may collect server logs including IP addresses, browser type, and referring URLs for security and infrastructure purposes.

Please review GitHub's Privacy Statement for full details on how GitHub handles this infrastructure data.

📌 This logging is performed by GitHub (our hosting provider), not by Wondermayank. We have no access to these server logs.

🍪

Cookies

Wondermayank itself does not set tracking cookies. However, the following third-party services may set cookies when you visit:

  • Google Analytics: Sets anonymous measurement cookies (_ga, _gid) to track sessions. These do not identify you personally and expire automatically.
  • Google Fonts: May log font requests to serve typography. See Google's Privacy Policy for details.
  • Theme preference: Your dark/light mode choice is stored in your browser's localStorage — not a cookie, and never sent to any server.

🍃 No cookies are used to track, profile, or identify you personally. You can clear cookies at any time from your browser settings without affecting your experience on this site.

🔗

Third-Party Services

Our site integrates with these third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Google Analytics 4 — anonymous traffic measurement
  • Google Fonts — web font delivery
  • YouTube — embedded educational video content
  • Telegram — community links and bot support contact
  • TMDB / Jikan APIs — movie and anime data for finder tools
  • Google Sheets (Apps Script) — used in ThunderStudy exam result recording (optional, user-initiated)

We do not control these services and are not responsible for their data practices. Please review their respective privacy policies before interacting with embedded features.

🌐 External links on Wondermayank are provided for convenience. We do not endorse or control the privacy practices of any external website.

Children's Privacy

Wondermayank is designed to be a safe, educational resource suitable for students of all ages. We are committed to child safety online:

  • We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.
  • No registration or personal information is required to use any part of this site.
  • All content is educational and age-appropriate.

If you believe a child has inadvertently submitted personal information through our site, please contact us immediately via Telegram at @Mindmaster_robot and we will promptly address it.
