10. Local Storage, Data & Privacy

ThunderStudy is designed to be 100% safe and local-first. Here is exactly how your data is handled:

  • Browser localStorage — Progress, scores, and preferences (like dark/light mode) are saved only in your own browser. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.
  • No accounts, no passwords — There is no registration system. We cannot access any personal information because we never collect it.
  • No tracking cookies — ThunderStudy itself sets no identifying cookies. Google Analytics uses anonymous measurement cookies (_ga, _gid) that do not identify you personally.
  • No data sold — We do not sell, share, or monetize any user data.
  • GitHub Pages logging — The hosting provider (GitHub) may log technical metadata (IP address, browser type) for security purposes. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.

🛡️ No hack, no risk: Since ThunderStudy doesn't collect personal data and all progress is local to your browser, there is nothing to steal or breach. Your study data is yours alone.

For the full privacy details, please read our Privacy Policy.
